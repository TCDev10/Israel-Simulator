# Israel-Simulator — Open-World Minecraft Expansion

**Israel-Simulator** is a rich open-world sandbox mod for **NeoForge 26.2** (Minecraft 26.2, Java 25) that transforms the Minecraft landscape into an immersive journey through Israeli biomes, living historical and modern cities, cultural traditions, unique economies, and chaotic sandbox encounters.

---

## 🌟 Key Highlights

* **Procedural Geography & Clustered Biomes**: Realistic West-to-East terrain transitions across Mediterranean Coast, Urban Metropolitan areas, Agricultural olive groves & vineyards, Jerusalem Highlands, Judean Desert, and the mineral-rich Dead Sea salt flats.
* **16 Fully Furnished Real Structures**: Explore the sacred Western Wall plaza & underground tunnels, Tel Aviv cityscapes, modular Jerusalem Old City bazaars, Grand Markets, Government Buildings, Ancient Sanctuaries, Synagogues, and Coastal Ports.
* **Tel Aviv District System**: 6 functional districts (Startup, Rothschild, Florentin, Sarona, Promenade, White City) with real-time HUD announcements and regional trade perks.
* **Living Cultural Mechanics**:
  * Pray at the Western Wall with a Kippah and Prayer Note for diamond rewards and the Blessed Effect.
  * Light the Hanukkah Menorah progressively candle by candle.
  * Experience the Kosher food digestion mechanics and holiday world events.
* **32 Custom Items & Authentic Cuisine**: Minted Shekel & Agora coins, Rav-Kav transport cards, and authentic culinary treats including Falafel, Shakshuka, Sabich, Hummus, Challah, Matzo, Sufganiyot, and fresh Grapes.
* **Mythic Collectibles & Boss Battles**: Seek out the mythic *Rabbi's Crown*, discover the *First Amendment*, play the legendary *Hava Nagila* music disc, or challenge the satirical *Bibi* boss and his security guards.

---

## 📦 Requirements & Installation

1. Install **Minecraft Java Edition 26.2**.
2. Install **NeoForge 26.2.0.88** or newer.
3. Requires **Java 25**.
4. Download `israel_simulator-0.1.0.jar` and place it in your `.minecraft/mods` folder.

---

## 👥 Multiplayer & Dedicated Servers

Israel-Simulator is built from the ground up for multiplayer servers! All interactions, timers, and reward claims are strictly authoritative on the server side with zero client-side cheat vulnerabilities.

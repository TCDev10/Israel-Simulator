# Israel-Simulator

Israel in Minecraft. A NeoForge mod for Minecraft 26.2 (Java 25) that adds Israeli biomes, cities and landmarks to normal world generation, plus food, money, religious stuff and a satirical boss fight.

---

## What's in it

- **6 biomes**, roughly west to east: Mediterranean Coast, Urban Area (Tel Aviv), farmland with olive groves and vineyards, Jerusalem, the Judean Desert and the Dead Sea.
- **19 structures**, all furnished and with their own loot: the Western Wall, Jerusalem's Old City, a Mahane Yehuda-style market, synagogues, Tel Aviv, the Knesset, a startup office, Jaffa port, a moshav, desert ruins, Ein Gedi, a Dead Sea resort, Gaza, the Twin Towers and more.
- **Tel Aviv districts**: 6 of them (Startup District, Rothschild, Florentin, Sarona, Tayelet, White City), each with a banner when you walk in and its own trade prices.
- **Religion and holidays**:
  - Pray at the Western Wall with a Kippah and a Prayer Note for diamonds and the Blessed effect.
  - Light the Menorah one candle at a time.
  - Kosher rules: don't mix meat and dairy.
  - A holiday calendar with Shabbat and the main Jewish holidays. The Shuk changes stock and closes on Shabbat.
- **70 items and blocks**: Shekels and Agorot, the Rav-Kav card, falafel, shakshuka, sabich, hummus, challah, matzo, sufganiyot, grapes, guns, a bicycle and more.
- **Bosses**: summon Bibi with the Star of David. He's the only source of the Rabbi's Crown and drops the Hava Nagila music disc. Get the First Amendment at the public speech event.
- **Also**: a Tel Aviv to Jerusalem train, Mossad missions, a plane crash event (one plane a minute at the twin towers when a player is nearby), local animals.

---

## Install

1. Minecraft Java Edition 26.2.
2. NeoForge 26.2.0.88 or newer.
3. Java 25.
4. Put `israel_simulator-0.1.0.jar` in your `.minecraft/mods` folder.

---

## Multiplayer

Works on servers. Install it on the server and on every client. Prayers, rewards, trades and bosses all run server-side.

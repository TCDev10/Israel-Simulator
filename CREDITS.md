## 3. Visual Artwork & Textures
- **Pixel Art & 3D Item Models**:
  - Rabbi's Crown, Kippah, Talit, Tefillin 3D models and textures: Original creations for Israel-Simulator.
  - Bibi Boss & Coalition Guard entity skins: Original satirical pixel art for Israel-Simulator.
  - Menorah block model & texture: Original multi-candle Hanukkah menorah model.
  - Culinary & collectible icons: Hand-crafted 16x16 pixel art.
  - All textures created without AI image generators or unauthorized copyrighted assets.

## 4. Audio & Musical Heritage
- **Folk & Liturgical Melodies**:
  - *Hava Nagila*: Traditional Jewish folk melody (composed in the public domain by Abraham Zevi Idelsohn / Moshe Nathanson).
  - *Klezmer*: Traditional Ashkenazi folk dance motifs in the public domain.
  - *Shabbat Shalom*: Traditional liturgical Sabbath greeting songs in the public domain.
- **Sound Design**:
  - Sound effects utilize Minecraft vanilla sound references and synthesizers, adhering to the Mojang EULA.
  - No copyrighted commercial recordings or performances are distributed with this software.

---

## 5. Cultural & Historical Inspiration
- Inspired by the vibrant culture, Mediterranean landscapes, desert biomes, ancient history, cuisine, and celebrations of Israel and the Levant.
- Fictionalized and satirical elements (such as the Bibi Boss) are lighthearted parody and game mechanics, not historical statements.
